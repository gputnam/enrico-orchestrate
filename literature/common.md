# Common background: neutrino–nucleus interactions in the few-GeV region

The loop studies νμ charged-current scattering on hydrocarbon (CH) in the NuMI medium-energy beam
(⟨Eν⟩ ≈ 6 GeV, MINERvA). At these energies, quasielastic scattering (CCQE), multinucleon (2p2h/MEC),
resonant and non-resonant pion production, and shallow/deep inelastic scattering all contribute. The
nuclear ground state, medium effects (RPA), and final-state interactions (FSI) of the outgoing hadrons
shape every observable. Measurements are defined by **final-state topology** (e.g. "QE-like" = no
mesons) because the interaction channel itself is not observable.

- J.A. Formaggio & G.P. Zeller, "From eV to EeV: Neutrino Cross Sections Across Energy Scales", Rev. Mod. Phys. 84, 1307 (2012) — arXiv:1305.7513 — standard overview; vocabulary of QE/RES/DIS.
- L. Alvarez-Ruso et al. (NuSTEC), "NuSTEC White Paper: Status and challenges of neutrino–nucleus scattering", Prog. Part. Nucl. Phys. 100, 1 (2018) — arXiv:1706.03621 — what is known/unknown in nuclear modelling, generators, measurements.
- T. Katori & M. Martini, "Neutrino–nucleus cross sections for oscillation experiments", J. Phys. G 45, 013001 (2018) — arXiv:1611.07770 — compact review of QE, 2p2h and pion physics.
- U. Mosel, "Neutrino Interactions with Nucleons and Nuclei: Importance for Long-Baseline Experiments", Ann. Rev. Nucl. Part. Sci. 66, 171 (2016) — arXiv:1602.00696 — nuclear effects and FSI and how they bias energy reconstruction.
- H. Gallagher, G. Garvey, G.P. Zeller, "Neutrino-nucleus interactions", Ann. Rev. Nucl. Part. Sci. 61, 355 (2011) — concise review of channels and the experimental landscape.
- K. Mahn, C. Marshall, C. Wilkinson, "Progress in Measurements of 0.1–10 GeV Neutrino–Nucleus Scattering and Anticipated Results from Future Experiments", Ann. Rev. Nucl. Part. Sci. 68, 105 (2018) — arXiv:1803.08848 — measurement landscape including MINERvA.
- O. Benhar et al., "Neutrino–nucleus interactions and the determination of oscillation parameters", Phys. Rept. 700, 1 (2017) — arXiv:1501.06448 — nuclear-model systematics and their impact.
- L. Alvarez Ruso et al., "Theoretical tools for neutrino scattering: interplay between lattice QCD, EFTs, nuclear physics, phenomenology, and neutrino event generators", J. Phys. G 52, 043001 (2025) — arXiv:2203.09030 — map of the theory tool chain up to generators.
- A.M. Ankowski et al., "Electron scattering and neutrino physics", J. Phys. G 50, 120501 (2023) — arXiv:2203.06853 — electron scattering as a test of the vector response of nuclear models.
- A.B. Balantekin et al., "Snowmass Neutrino Frontier: Neutrino Interaction Cross Sections (NF06) Topical Group Report" — arXiv:2209.06872 — recent community priorities.
