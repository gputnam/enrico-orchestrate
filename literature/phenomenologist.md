# Phenomenologist: confronting models with a corpus of measurements

Your role is to judge data–model agreement across many correlated measurements and to diagnose
which physics is responsible: missing channels, nuclear effects or FSI. From that you decide
whether the next step is a model improvement or a new, discriminating measurement.

## Comparing models with unfolded, correlated data
- P. Stowell et al., "NUISANCE: a neutrino cross-section generator tuning and comparison framework", JINST 12, P01016 (2017) — arXiv:1612.07393.
- M. Buizza Avanzini et al., "Comparisons and challenges of modern neutrino-scattering experiments", Phys. Rev. D 105, 092004 (2022) — arXiv:2112.09194 — TENSIONS2019: cross-experiment tensions and χ² pitfalls.
- G. D'Agostini, "On the use of the covariance matrix to fit correlated data", Nucl. Instrum. Meth. A 346, 306 (1994) — Peelle's pertinent puzzle.
- W. Tang et al., "Data Unfolding with Wiener-SVD Method", JINST 12, P10002 (2017) — arXiv:1705.03568.
- L. Koch & S. Dolan, "Treatment of flux shape uncertainties in unfolded, flux-averaged neutrino cross-section measurements", Phys. Rev. D 102, 113012 (2020) — arXiv:2009.00552.

## Global tests of interaction models against many measurements
- C. Wilkinson et al., "Testing charged current quasi-elastic and multinucleon interaction models in the NEUT neutrino interaction generator with published datasets from the MiniBooNE and MINERνA experiments", Phys. Rev. D 93, 072010 (2016) — arXiv:1601.05592.
- S. Dolan, "Exploring Nuclear Effects in Neutrino-Nucleus Interactions Using Measurements of Transverse Kinematic Imbalance from T2K and MINERvA" — arXiv:1810.06043 — which observables discriminate between models.
- S. Dolan, G.D. Megias, S. Bolognesi, "Implementation of the SuSAv2-meson exchange current 1p1h and 2p2h models in GENIE and analysis of nuclear effects in T2K measurements", Phys. Rev. D 101, 033003 (2020) — arXiv:1905.08556.

## Why model adequacy matters
- A.M. Ankowski & C. Mariani, "Systematic uncertainties in long-baseline neutrino-oscillation experiments", J. Phys. G 44, 054001 (2017) — arXiv:1609.00258.
- J. Chakrani et al., "Parametrized uncertainties in the spectral function model of neutrino charged-current quasielastic interactions for oscillation analyses", Phys. Rev. D 109, 072006 (2024) — arXiv:2308.01838.

Going further: when a discrepancy pattern appears, search the physics literature for the mechanisms
that produce it, and follow citations of the relevant MINERvA papers on INSPIRE. Judge the model
against the data only. Never use other generators' predictions (GENIE/MnvTune, NuWro, NEUT, GiBUU)
as a benchmark.
